# Guided Play Overhaul

A guided play overhaul for Hinterland, built around all 9 Steam achievements. A small window over the game works out your next move from the game's own rules and points to it with a compass arrow, and helpers you can switch on take over the fighting, the potions and the clicks.

- One step at a time: what to fight, where, and in what order, with every fight rated SAFE, OK, RISKY or NO from the game's combat rules.
- Gear: which item to wear, judged over every enemy left on the map. Junk to sell flashes red in your inventories.
- Healing: when to rest in town and when to drink a potion, never one that would be wasted.
- Loot: the chests and bodies worth opening on your way.
- Town plan: who to hire, what to upgrade, build and research, up to a full town of 28 buildings. Food workers are traded up until you have 6 Farmers on 6 Plantations.
- Raids, the king's requests, wanderers, and the last site, which waits until the achievements that need it are done.
- Which achievements you already have is read from Steam.

## Settings

Press F8 to switch between one step only, the step with details, and hidden. Drag the window to move it.

- Text size
- Auto-attack (F7 also switches it): fights back against enemies attacking you, mages first, then archers, then melee. Attacks the enemy the guide sends you to once you reach its site, defends the town against raiders, and steps around trees and fences when you get stuck. While you run from a fight it only hits back when you stand still, and never chases.
- Screen WASD: W, A, S, D and the arrow keys move you up, left, down and right on the screen instead of along the map. Unticked, the arrow keys move you like the game's own WASD.
- Wheel zoom: the mouse wheel zooms the camera in and out. On the town map it zooms the town map, so every building lot fits on screen.
- Auto Potion: drinks a Healing Potion when you drop under half HP, never in town.
- Potion buttons: a bar above the tick boxes with one button for each potion you would otherwise drop on your portrait (Regeneration, Harden, Berserker, bloods and more). Click one to drink it.

## Install

1. Copy the `tools` folder into the Hinterland game folder (Steam: `Steam\steamapps\common\Hinterland`).
2. Start the game. The game already loads `tools\main.cs` on its own, so no game file is changed.

To remove it, delete the `tools` folder.
